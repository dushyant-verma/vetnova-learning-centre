PURPOSE & LEADERSHIP

Founder's Vision

Why VetNova was created and how we are redefining practical veterinary education in India.

CLINICIAN-FOUNDED INSTITUTE

Bridging the Tactile Gap in Veterinary Medicine

"True surgical dexterity and diagnostic confidence cannot be acquired through textbooks alone. Every veterinarian deserves a safe, mentored environment where they perform procedures independently before stepping into solo practice."

VetNova was born out of a stark clinical reality observed by practicing veterinary specialists and hospital directors: fresh graduates possess strong theoretical foundations, yet face immense anxiety during their first independent soft tissue surgeries, emergency triages, and diagnostic ultrasound scans due to limited hands-on practice in traditional college curriculums.

We established VetNova to pioneer small-batch, scenario-first training where every doctor performs procedure steps independently under direct 1-on-1 specialist guidance. Our broader mission is to raise the baseline standard of veterinary healthcare across India and build a compassionate, highly competent clinical community.

INSTITUTIONAL MILESTONES

Our Journey

How VetNova evolved from a vision into India's premier practical veterinary learning center.

2020 • Genesis

Identifying The Practical Learning Gap

Founding specialists and clinic owners recognized a critical dilemma: fresh veterinary graduates possess strong theoretical grounding, yet face anxiety during their first independent soft tissue surgeries and diagnostic cases due to insufficient hands-on practice in college curriculums.

2021 • Innovation

Engineering Scenario-First Modules

We engineered small-batch, scenario-first clinical modules where every doctor executes procedures independently. By maintaining low student-to-faculty ratios, trainees build real manual dexterity and decision-making muscle memory under direct specialist supervision.

2023 • Expansion

Establishing Wagholi Pune Campus

Inaugurated our dedicated 3,000 sq. ft. practical facility in Gawadewadi – Wagholi, Pune, complete with a surgical procedure wet lab, digital X-ray & ultrasound diagnostic suite, smart AV lecture theatre, and emergency handling zones.

2026 & Beyond • Leadership

Empowering 3,000+ Veterinarians

Having trained over 3,000 veterinarians and nurses, VetNova continues setting the national benchmark for ethical, welfare-blended clinical skill enhancement across small animal practice, emergency care, and advanced surgical specialties.
