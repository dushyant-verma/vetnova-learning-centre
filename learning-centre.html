STEP-BY-STEP PATH

Your Practical Learning Journey

How trainees progress from basic orientation to independent procedural execution.

STAGE 1 • DAY 1

Orientation & Scrub Protocol

Campus tour, sterile OR gowning familiarization, and surgical instrument ergonomics.

STAGE 2 • MODULE 1

Tactile Model Practice

Executing suture patterns, vessel ligation, and incision closures on anatomical simulation models.

STAGE 3 • MODULE 2

Guided Mentor Demonstration

Observing step-by-step surgical and ultrasound scanning procedures conducted live by senior faculty.

STAGE 4 • MODULE 3

Independent Execution

Performing procedural steps under direct 1-on-1 mentor supervision with instant feedback.

STAGE 5 • GRADUATION

Evaluation & Certification

Demonstrating skill competency during practical capstone assessment and receiving verified certificate credentials.

CLINICAL HARDWARE

Modern Equipment & Technology

Trainees practice using standard clinical hardware deployed in modern veterinary hospitals.

Digital Ultrasound Scanning Units

Multi-frequency micro-convex & linear probes for abdominal, cardiac, and FAST fluid detection scans.

High-Frequency Digital X-Ray

Digital radiography CR/DR systems for instant thoracic, abdominal, and orthopedic radiograph evaluation.

Multiparameter Vitals Monitors

Real-time ECG, SpO2, NIBP, end-tidal CO2, and temperature monitoring during anesthetic procedures.

Sterile Surgical Packs

Complete soft tissue instrument sets including Mayo-Hegar needle drivers, Crile hemstats, and scalpel handles.

Emergency Resuscitation Kits

Endotracheal intubation tubes, laryngoscopes, ambu bags, and emergency drug dosing stations.

Isoflurane Anesthetic Vaporizers

Precision precision gas anesthetic delivery systems with oxygen flush and scavenger setups.
