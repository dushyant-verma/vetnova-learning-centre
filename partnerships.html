COLLABORATION DOMAINS

Key Partnership Opportunities

VetNova collaborates across specialized veterinary domains to advance practical skill standards, clinical confidence, and professional education.

Hospital & Clinical Partners

Connecting veterinary practitioners with real-world clinical rotations, surgical procedures, and referral hospital networks.

  • Clinical rotation pathways
  • Live procedural shadowing
  • Surgical & case-based learning

Medical Equipment & Instrument Partners

Equipping hands-on wet labs with state-of-the-art surgical instruments, digital X-rays, ultrasound probes, and ICU monitors.

  • Modern OT instrument wet labs
  • Diagnostic probe handling workshops
  • Advanced technology demonstrations

Diagnostic Laboratories & Pathology Partners

Collaborating with pathology reference labs and diagnostic experts to provide advanced interpretation and case-based training.

  • Clinical pathology panels
  • Radiograph reading workshops
  • Diagnostic lab workflows

Academic & Research Institutions

Partnering with veterinary universities, colleges, and research bodies to complement theoretical curricula with practical skill validation.

  • Practical curriculum alignment
  • Knowledge & faculty exchanges
  • Evidence-based clinical research

Animal Welfare NGOs & Community Partners

Partnering with local animal welfare organizations, shelters, and municipal bodies for free spay/neuter drives and community health camps.

  • High-volume ABC/ARV surgical drives
  • Community outreach initiatives
  • Humane animal handling standards
