ETHICAL VETERINARY CARE

Our Commitment to Animal Welfare

Veterinary medicine is not just about procedural dexterity—it is a moral calling to safeguard animal dignity, alleviate pain, and promote public awareness regarding humane pet stewardship.

Ethical Treatment & Pain Management Prioritizing pre-emptive analgesia and gentle, fear-free patient handling during every diagnostic and surgical procedure.
Preventive Healthcare Protocols Promoting routine vaccination, parasite control, and early health screenings to prevent avoidable animal suffering.
Welfare Education for Practitioners Integrating animal welfare science, bioethics, and legal standards into all VetNova clinical training modules.
Community Outreach & Rescue Support Partnering with local animal welfare NGOs, shelters, and rescuers to provide free health camps and emergency treatment.
CORE PRINCIPLES

Animal Welfare Pillars

Six foundational pillars guiding every workshop, health camp, and clinical program at VetNova.

Compassionate Care

Standardizing fear-free restraint, gentle patient handling, and minimal-stress environment protocols across hospital practices.

Preventive Medicine

Advocating comprehensive rabies eradication, core vaccination schedules, and early disease detection to safeguard community health.

Rescue & Rehabilitation

Providing pro-bono surgical care, emergency triage support, and wound management for stray and rescued animals in need.

Responsible Pet Ownership

Educating pet parents on balanced nutrition, exercise, dental care, and behavioral understanding for long-term pet wellbeing.

Community Awareness

Conducting public workshops, school awareness programs, and anti-rabies drives to build empathetic, animal-friendly neighborhoods.

Ethical Veterinary Practice

Upholding veterinary bioethics, informed consent, transparent client counseling, and continuous professional development standards.

ON-THE-GROUND IMPACT

Welfare Initiatives & Outreach

How VetNova active campaigns translate compassionate principles into tangible community impact.

CAMPAIGN 1 • PUBLIC HEALTH

Free Rabies Vaccination Drives

Organizing annual mass anti-rabies vaccination camps in partnership with municipal bodies and local animal welfare groups across Pune.

CAMPAIGN 2 • RURAL REACH

Rural Animal Health Camps

Deploying mobile veterinary teams to rural areas to conduct health checkups, deworming drives, and livestock management counseling.

CAMPAIGN 3 • EMERGENCY CARE

Rescue Surgical Support

Offering subsidised and pro-bono soft tissue surgeries for injured stray animals brought in by registered animal shelters and rescuers.

CAMPAIGN 4 • PET PARENT EDUCATION

Pet First Aid Workshops

Hosting free weekend workshops for pet owners to learn life-saving emergency stabilization techniques before reaching vet clinics.

CAMPAIGN 5 • COLLABORATION

Welfare NGO Partnerships

Collaborating with leading animal protection organizations to train shelter managers and assistants in hygienic animal care protocols.
