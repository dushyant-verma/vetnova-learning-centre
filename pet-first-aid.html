HANDS-ON LEARNING MODULES

What You Will Master at the Workshop

Every station provides direct simulation experience so you react calmly and swiftly in critical moments.

Canine & Feline CPR

Practice chest compressions at 100–120 bpm, mouth-to-snout ventilation, and cardiac arrest recognition using dedicated animal CPR mannequins.

Choking & Airway Clearing

Learn finger-sweep safety techniques, modified Heimlich maneuvers for small and large dogs, and how to safely inspect an obstructed airway.

Bleeding & Pressure Bandaging

Apply pressure dressings, arterial pressure points, tourniquet protocols when indicated, and paw/limb bandaging that stays secure in transit.

Heatstroke & Seizure Protocol

Safely manage acute hyperthermia without causing shock, understand active evaporative cooling, and protect pets during violent epileptic seizures.

Poison & Toxin Emergencies

Immediate steps for chocolate, rodenticide, paracetamol, and lily poisoning: when to induce vomiting, when NOT to induce vomiting, and poison control protocol.

Safe Handling & Stretcher Transport

Create makeshift muzzles, immobilize suspected spinal and pelvic fractures, and transfer injured or panicking animals into vehicles safely.

PARTICIPANTS

Who Should Attend This Workshop?

Designed for anyone who shares their life or work with companion animals.

Pet Parents & Guardians

Protect your dog or cat during home accidents, choking emergencies, outdoor encounters, and travel mishaps.

Animal Rescuers & Shelter Staff

Handle road traffic accidents, collapse, dehydration, and trauma triage in the field before clinical intake.

Pet Sitters, Boarders & Groomers

Provide peace of mind to clients and ensure professional emergency readiness in boarding kennels and salons.
