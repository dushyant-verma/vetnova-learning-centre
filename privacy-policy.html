1 Introduction

VetNova Learning Centre ("VetNova", "we", "our", or "us") respects visitor privacy and is committed to protecting personal information submitted through our website and digital channels.

This Privacy Policy outlines our policies, standards, and practices regarding the collection, use, protection, storage, and handling of personal data obtained from visitors, prospective trainees, enrolled veterinarians, clinical staff, and general users of our website. By accessing or using the VetNova Learning Centre website, you acknowledge the terms described in this policy.

Commitment to Transparency: We operate with full transparency regarding data collection. We collect only the minimum necessary information required to deliver high-quality clinical training, respond to academic inquiries, and process course admissions.

2 Information We Collect

We collect information that visitors voluntarily submit when interacting with our website, as well as certain technical information automatically gathered by our web hosting and security infrastructure.

Voluntarily Provided Information: You may provide personal information through:

  • Contact forms and general enquiry submissions
  • Counselling and academic admission request forms
  • Programme application and seat reservation forms
  • Clinical workshop registrations and event sign-ups
  • Newsletter subscriptions and knowledge hub updates
  • Course brochure or curriculum syllabus download requests
  • Direct phone calls, WhatsApp messages, or email communications

The information submitted may include, but is not limited to:

  • Contact Details: Full name, email address, mobile number, and phone number
  • Location Data: City, state, country, and postal code
  • Professional Data: Educational background, BVSc / MVSc status, registration numbers, clinical experience, or veterinary clinic details
  • Enquiry Specifics: Batch preferences, course interest areas, accommodation requirements, and submitted questions

Automatically Collected Technical Information:

When you visit our website, our servers and analytics tools may automatically record standard technical data, including your IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device identifiers, referring website addresses, pages viewed, time spent per page, and general site navigation paths.

3 How We Use Information

Information collected by VetNova Learning Centre is used strictly for legitimate educational, academic, and administrative purposes, including:

  • Responding to course enquiries, admission questions, and consultation requests
  • Processing application submissions and batch allocations for clinical training programmes
  • Providing personalized academic counselling regarding veterinary skill tracks
  • Communicating schedule notifications, venue updates, and pre-workshop preparation materials
  • Delivering requested course brochures, curriculum outlines, and learning materials
  • Improving website performance, navigation structure, and overall user experience
  • Maintaining website security, identifying technical issues, and preventing unauthorized activity
  • Analyzing aggregated visitor statistics and institutional reporting
  • Sending relevant educational newsletters or upcoming workshop announcements where explicit opt-in consent has been provided

4 Cookies & Web Tracking

The VetNova Learning Centre website may use cookies and similar web tracking technologies to ensure smooth website functionality and improve your browsing experience.

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by web pages you visit. We utilize cookies for:

  • Essential Functionality: Ensuring page navigation, form submittal integrity, and layout consistency
  • Preferences & Sessions: Remembering user settings and navigation preferences
  • Analytics & Performance: Understanding visitor interactions and measuring page load speeds
  • User Experience: Delivering optimized media and dynamic content rendering

Managing Cookies: Most web browsers allow you to manage cookie preferences through their settings menu. You can set your browser to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the performance or functionality of specific website features.

5 Analytics & Third-Party Services

We may utilize trusted third-party service providers to assist in operating our website, delivering communications, processing forms, and analyzing website traffic.

These third-party service partners have access to personal information only to perform specific tasks on our behalf and are obligated under strict confidentiality terms not to disclose or use it for any other purpose. We do not authorise third-party providers to use your personal data for their own independent marketing or commercial purposes.

6 Data Sharing & Disclosure

VetNova Learning Centre does not sell, rent, trade, or lease personal information to third parties.

We may share collected information only under the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With vetted hosting partners, technology infrastructure providers, and communication channels required for website operation and course coordination
  • Academic Staff: With authorized admissions counselors, faculty, and administrative personnel assisting with your admission or workshop registration
  • Legal Obligations: When disclosure is required by applicable law, regulation, legal proceeding, or enforceable governmental demand
  • Rights Protection: When necessary to enforce our site policies, protect our legal rights, or guarantee the safety and security of VetNova trainees, faculty, or the general public

7 Data Security

We implement reasonable technical, organizational, and physical security measures to safeguard personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Our security practices include encrypted communications, access-restricted database systems, and routine server maintenance. However, please be aware that no electronic transmission over the Internet or data storage system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure. Visitors submit information over the Internet at their own risk.

8 Data Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the specific purposes for which it was collected, satisfy academic record-keeping requirements, resolve administrative inquiries, adhere to legal obligations, or fulfill legitimate educational business objectives.

When personal data is no longer required, it is securely deleted, anonymized, or disposed of in accordance with our operational standards.

9 Your Rights

Where applicable under applicable law, website visitors and trainees may contact VetNova Learning Centre to exercise the following data rights:

  • Access: Inquire about what personal information we hold regarding you
  • Correction: Request updates or corrections to inaccurate or incomplete personal details
  • Deletion: Request erasure or removal of your personal data where legally applicable
  • Opt-Out: Withdraw consent for optional email newsletters or promotional communications at any time
  • Inquiries: Ask questions regarding our data protection standards and practices

To exercise any of these rights, please submit a written request using the contact details provided at the bottom of this page.

10 Children's Privacy

The VetNova Learning Centre website and clinical training programmes are intended exclusively for professional veterinarians, veterinary students, and adult healthcare staff. Our website is not intended to knowingly collect or solicit unnecessary personal information from children under 18 years of age without appropriate parent or guardian consent.

11 External Links

Our website may contain hyperlinks to external websites, partner organizations, or industry references. VetNova Learning Centre is not responsible for the privacy policies, content, security, or practices of external third-party websites. We encourage users to read the privacy statements of every external website they visit.

12 Changes to This Policy

VetNova Learning Centre reserves the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our educational offerings, legal obligations, or technical infrastructure.

Any updates will be published directly on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage visitors to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we protect personal information.
